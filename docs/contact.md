# Contact, authorship and citation

`pynxtools-xrm` is developed at **ZBT – Zentrum für BrennstoffzellenTechnik GmbH**
by **Jonas Fortmann**. It is a ZBT community plugin that integrates with the
NOMAD/FAIRmat ecosystem, not a package authored by “The NOMAD Authors”.

For plugin issues include versions, the failing mainfile type, relevant logs
and a minimal anonymized reproducer. For account/server access contact the
administrator of your Oasis. Upstream NOMAD and pynxtools issues can be referred
to their respective projects after identifying the failing layer.

## Attribution and license

NOMAD, pynxtools and the NeXus definitions are independent upstream projects.
The plugin uses their APIs and template infrastructure. Retained NOMAD copyright
notices identify upstream/template portions; they do not identify the authors
or maintainers of this ZBT package. The package uses Apache-2.0; consult the
repository's `LICENSE` and any retained source notices.

No FAIRmat/DFG funding for this plugin is asserted here. Dependency funding and
institutional affiliations must not be copied into this project's metadata.
